Demographic profile of all 470 populated Maryland ZIP codes: household income, homeownership, home values, age and vehicles, built from the U.S. Census Bureau's ACS 2020-2024 five-year estimates (released December 2025), the most current ZIP-level data published. Baltimore & Central Maryland is your largest lead market and is called out throughout: Baltimore City, Baltimore County, Anne Arundel, Howard, Harford and Carroll. The Washington suburbs (Prince George's, Montgomery, Charles and Frederick) are your second market and are called out alongside. Section 07 lines the state up against the 585 GOAL Auto leads delivered from July 1 to November 28, 2025, 268 of them in Maryland; the 287 Virginia and 30 Delaware leads are covered there as counts. The companion spreadsheet carries every ZIP and every column; the hurricane and flood report covers the risk geography.

08

Data & files

  • SourceU.S. Census Bureau, American Community Survey 2020-2024 five-year estimates (released December 2025), tables B01002, B01003, B08201, B11001, B11007, B19013, B25001, B25002, B25003, B25004, B25077. Geography: 2020 ZIP Code Tabulation Areas.
  • Coverage470 populated Maryland ZIPs. PO-box-only ZIPs have no resident population and no Census profile; traffic from them resolves to the surrounding ZIP. Median income is published for 420 ZIPs and median home value for 428.
  • Top-codingThe Census caps medians at “$250,000 or more” for income (7 ZIPs) and “$2,000,000 or more” for home value (1 ZIP); those ZIPs are shown at the cap.
  • GeographyMaryland has 23 counties and one independent city, Baltimore City, which is not part of Baltimore County and is treated as its own county-equivalent, as the Census Bureau does; the county column in the spreadsheet reads “Baltimore city” or “Baltimore County”. Each ZIP is assigned to the jurisdiction holding the largest share of its land (2020 ZCTA-to-county relationship file); 1 ZIP whose land lies mostly in a neighboring state is left out. The 24 jurisdictions are grouped into six regions: Baltimore & Central Maryland, Washington Suburbs, Southern Maryland, Western Maryland, Upper Eastern Shore and Lower Eastern Shore. The city column is left blank: no city file was used, so ZIPs are identified by county.
  • LeadsAll 585 GOAL leads for this account, from its one Auto campaign, Jul 1 to Nov 28, 2025, counted by ZIP: 268 in Maryland, 287 in Virginia and 30 in Delaware. 265 of the 268 Maryland leads matched a populated Maryland ZIP. Virginia and Delaware ZIP data could not be downloaded for this report, so those leads are shown as counts. No geo-targeting changes have been made.
  • PrecisionAll values carry ACS margins of error and are strongest for larger ZIPs. Blank means not published, never zero.
  • FilesHughes-MD-ZIP-Data-10-28-26.csv · every Maryland ZIP, every column, with the hurricane and flood tier from the companion report.
